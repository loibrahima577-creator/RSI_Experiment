# Task 11: group_words

Implement `group_words(words)`. Group words by their first letter. Keep groups in the order their initial letters first appear, and keep words within each group in their input order.

Examples:
- `group_words(["apple", "berry", "apricot"])` creates an `a` group and a `b` group.
- The `a` group contains `"apple"` and `"apricot"`.
